Solar Streetlight Solutions

Autonomous Outdoor Lighting That Pays for Itself

Meneliech Energy's solar streetlights are fully self-contained lighting systems that harvest sunlight during the day and illuminate your streets, driveways, and public spaces all night long. No trenching, no cables, no electricity bills — just clean, reliable light wherever you need it.

Built with high-efficiency monocrystalline solar panels, long-life LiFePO4 batteries, and intelligent LED arrays with motion sensing, our streetlights automatically dim to save power when no one is around and brighten instantly when movement is detected. Perfect for estates, campuses, highways, and rural communities.

  • All-in-one integrated design
  • Smart motion & dusk-to-dawn sensors
  • 3-5 nights autonomy on full charge
  • 10+ year lifespan, zero maintenance

Why Solar Streetlights

Why Go Solar for Outdoor Lighting

Traditional street lighting means expensive trenching, cable theft, monthly bills, and constant maintenance. Solar streetlights eliminate all of that — permanently.

Zero Electricity Bills

Powered entirely by the sun. No grid connection means no monthly electricity costs — ever. Your lighting pays for itself in 2-3 years.

No Cable Theft Risk

All-in-one design with no external wiring eliminates cable theft — a major problem for conventional street lighting across Nigeria.

Zero Maintenance

Sealed LiFePO4 batteries, tempered glass solar panels, and automatic self-cleaning LED arrays mean install and forget for a decade.

Install Anywhere

No grid access needed. Install on remote roads, rural communities, estates, and parks without expensive infrastructure or permits.

Technical Details

Solar Streetlight Specifications

We use only IP65-rated, weatherproof components engineered for Nigeria's tropical climate. Every streetlight is tested for heat resistance, water ingress, and wind load before delivery.

Solar Panel

High-efficiency monocrystalline panel (60W-300W) with tempered glass and anti-reflective coating for maximum light capture

Battery System

Built-in LiFePO4 battery (12.8V/24V, 30Ah-200Ah) with 6,000+ cycles and intelligent temperature compensation

LED Array

Philips/OSRAM SMD LEDs (30W-200W) with 160+ lm/W efficiency, 50,000+ hour lifespan, and uniform light distribution

Smart Controller

MPPT charge controller with dusk-to-dawn auto switching, motion sensing, dimming profiles, and remote monitoring option

Warranty & Rating

IP65 weatherproof rating, wind resistance up to 150km/h, 3-5 year warranty on all components

Operating Time

12+ hours at full brightness, 3-5 nights autonomy on stored charge, automatic dimming to extend runtime

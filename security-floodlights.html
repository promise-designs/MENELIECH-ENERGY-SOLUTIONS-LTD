Security Floodlight Solutions

Motion-Activated Solar Lighting for Maximum Protection

Meneliech Energy's solar security floodlights combine high-intensity LED output with intelligent PIR motion sensors to create a powerful deterrent against intruders. When someone approaches your property, the floodlight instantly blazes to full brightness — flooding the area with light and alerting you to their presence.

Powered entirely by integrated solar panels and long-life LiFePO4 batteries, these units require zero wiring, zero electricity costs, and zero maintenance. Install them on walls, fences, gates, or poles to create a protective perimeter around homes, offices, warehouses, farms, and construction sites.

  • PIR motion detection up to 12 meters
  • 2,000-10,000 lumen output options
  • Dusk-to-dawn auto operation
  • IP65 weatherproof, anti-theft design

Why Solar Floodlights

Why Solar Security Lighting Beats Grid-Powered

Traditional security lights need wiring, increase your electricity bill, and fail during blackouts. Solar floodlights solve every one of these problems — permanently.

Works During Blackouts

Grid-powered security lights die when power fails — exactly when you need them most. Solar floodlights store their own energy and work 24/7, rain or shine.

Zero Operating Cost

No wiring, no electrician fees, no monthly electricity bills. Once installed, your security lighting is completely free to run for 10+ years.

Smart Motion Sensing

Advanced PIR sensors detect body heat up to 12 meters away, instantly triggering full brightness. Adjustable sensitivity and timing for your needs.

Install Anywhere in Minutes

No trenching, no cables, no electrical permits. Mount on any wall, fence, or pole with included hardware. Fully operational the same day.

Technical Details

Security Floodlight Specifications

Our security floodlights use military-grade components engineered for Nigeria's harsh environment. Every unit is sealed against dust and water, with anti-theft mounting and tamper-resistant design.

LED Output

High-intensity SMD LEDs (100W-500W equivalent) delivering 2,000-10,000 lumens with 120° wide beam angle for maximum coverage

PIR Motion Sensor

Adjustable PIR detection up to 12m range, 120° angle, with customizable illumination duration (10s-5min) and daylight override

Solar Panel

High-efficiency monocrystalline panel (25W-100W) with tempered glass, integrated into the housing or separate for flexible mounting

Battery System

LiFePO4 battery (12.8V, 12Ah-50Ah) with 6,000+ cycles, low-temperature protection, and intelligent charge management

Build Quality

IP65 weatherproof rating, die-cast aluminum housing, tempered glass lens, anti-corrosion coating, 3-5 year component warranty

Operating Modes

Motion-activated full brightness, dim standby mode (30% brightness), dusk-to-dawn constant, or custom scheduling via remote

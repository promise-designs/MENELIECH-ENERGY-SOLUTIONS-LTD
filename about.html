About Meneliech Energy

Who We Are

Abuja's Trusted Partner for Premium Solar & Smart Lighting Solutions

Meneliech Energy Solutions is a premier renewable energy company headquartered in Gwarimpa, Abuja. We specialize in the supply, distribution, and professional installation of high-capacity hybrid inverters, advanced lithium energy storage systems, automated solar streetlights, and heavy-duty security floodlights. Our mission is simple: to deliver clean, silent, and uninterrupted 24/7 power to homes, corporate offices, estates, and industrial facilities across Nigeria.

Hybrid Inverter Solutions

Smart Power Management for Maximum Efficiency

Meneliech Energy's hybrid inverters are the intelligent core of every solar installation we deploy. These advanced units don't just convert DC solar power to usable AC electricity — they intelligently manage power flow between your solar panels, battery bank, grid supply, and loads in real time.

With built-in MPPT charge controllers, pure sine wave output, and seamless 10ms grid switching, our hybrid inverters deliver uninterrupted power that protects sensitive electronics while maximizing your solar harvest and minimizing grid dependence.

Why Hybrid Inverters

Why Hybrid Beats Stand-Alone Inverters

Traditional inverters only convert power. Hybrid inverters think, optimize, and adapt — delivering superior performance, lower bills, and true energy independence.

Intelligent Power Routing

Automatically prioritizes solar power, then battery, then grid — minimizing grid consumption and maximizing your free solar energy usage.

10ms Seamless Switching

Grid failure? Your inverter switches to battery backup in 10 milliseconds — fast enough that computers, servers, and medical equipment never notice.

Smart App Control

Monitor production, consumption, battery status, and grid usage in real time. Control settings remotely from your phone, anywhere in the world.

Lower Electricity Bills

Grid-tie capability lets you sell excess solar back to the grid where allowed, while time-of-use optimization charges batteries during cheap off-peak hours.

Hybrid Inverter Specifications

We partner with globally-certified manufacturers to supply inverters engineered for Nigeria's challenging power environment. Every unit is tested for voltage fluctuation tolerance, surge protection, and tropical climate resilience.

Conversion Efficiency

98%+ peak efficiency with pure sine wave output for safe operation of all appliances and sensitive electronics

MPPT Controller

Built-in MPPT with 99.9% tracking efficiency, wide PV input voltage range (90-550V), and multi-string support

Battery Compatibility

Universal compatibility with LiFePO4, lead-acid, and gel batteries with auto-detection and optimized charging profiles

Surge Capacity

2x rated power for 5 seconds — handles motor starting loads like AC compressors, pumps, and refrigerators effortlessly

Warranty Coverage

5-10 year manufacturer warranty on electronics, plus 1 year installation and commissioning warranty

Remote Monitoring

WiFi/4G connectivity with mobile app and web portal for real-time monitoring, alerts, and firmware updates
